RoomLab is an original teaching demo inspired by ArchMorph. Drag furniture to move it within a room. Furniture uses preset approximate metre dimensions; overlaps and out-of-room placements are rejected. No building-code, circulation or structural checks. State resets on reload. Demo setup guide
